# Build 5.3 - Athletics + People Who Know Me

Build 5.3 turns two more Home tiles into real student record modules without changing the SwiftData schema.

## Athletics
Students can now save:
- sport or team
- school / team / club
- position, event, or level
- season or milestone date
- stats, milestones, and important notes
- reflection on what they are proud of or working on
- Keepsakes: photos and files

## People Who Know Me
Students can now save:
- teacher, coach, counselor, mentor, employer, community leader, or other person
- school / organization
- how the person knows them
- last-connected date
- shared work / experiences
- what the person might remember about the student
- recommendation status
- Keepsakes: photos and files

## App integration
Both modules now automatically work with:
- Home record counts
- Quick Add (Add instead of Open)
- global Search
- record detail / edit / delete
- Keepsakes attachments

## Data model
No SwiftData schema change. These modules reuse the existing `ModuleRecord` model introduced in Build 5.
